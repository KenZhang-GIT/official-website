05 / Frequently Asked Questions
What industries do Repute Filtration products serve?

Our solutions are primarily designed for high-purity and heavy industrial sectors, including semiconductor fabrication, pharmaceutical processing, food and beverage clarification, chemical synthesis, and industrial water treatment.

Do you offer customized filter housings and OEM solutions?

Yes. We provide complete design, engineering, and manufacturing services for non-standard pressure vessels, high-flow housing configurations, and custom OEM cartridge media tailored to specific process conditions.

How do you ensure the quality of your filter cartridges?

All cleanroom-manufactured cartridges undergo rigorous integrity testing, non-fiber-releasing checks, and end-of-line quality validation to comply with global GMP and ISO criteria.

What is the typical lead time for international orders?

Standard filter cartridges and stocked housings typically ship within 1 to 2 weeks. Custom-engineered pressure vessels and automated filtration systems range from 4 to 8 weeks depending on engineering requirements.
